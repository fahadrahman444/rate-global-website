1. Hero Section
Must-have

The first section visitors see. Clearly communicate what your company does and why clients should choose you.

    A strong headline, such as “We Build High-Performing Websites for Growing Businesses”

    A short description of your services

    Primary CTA: Get a Free Quote

    Secondary CTA: View Our Work

2. Services Section
Must-have

Show exactly what clients can hire you to do.

    Website design and development

    Custom web application development

    E-commerce development

    UI/UX design

    Website redesign and maintenance

    SEO and website performance optimization

Give each service a short description and a dedicated details link.

3. Portfolio / Our Work
Must-have

Demonstrate your actual skills through real projects.

    Project screenshots or live website previews

    Client or project name

    Technologies used

    Your role and the problem you solved

    Live project link

Tip: A few detailed case studies are more convincing than a large gallery of screenshots.

4. About Us
Must-have

Introduce your company and explain what makes it different.

    Company overview

    Mission and vision

    Experience and expertise

    Team introduction

    Company values

5. Technologies & Expertise
Recommended

Show the tools and technologies your team genuinely works with.

    Frontend: React, Next.js, Vue

    Backend: Node.js, Laravel, Django

    CMS: WordPress, Shopify

    Database: PostgreSQL, MySQL, MongoDB

    Deployment: AWS, Vercel, cloud platforms

Only list technologies your team can confidently deliver with.

6. Our Process
Recommended

Explain how you take a project from idea to launch.

    Discovery and requirements

    Planning and strategy

    UI/UX design

    Development

    Testing and quality assurance

    Launch and ongoing support

This helps clients understand what working with your company will look like.

7. Testimonials & Trust Signals
Recommended

Reduce uncertainty for potential clients.

    Genuine client reviews

    Client logos, with permission

    Measurable project results

    Relevant certifications or partnerships

    Verified experience and achievements

If you're new, use real demo projects and clearly label them rather than inventing client testimonials.

8. Contact / Get a Quote
Must-have

Make it easy for visitors to start a conversation.

    Name and email

    Company or project name

    Type of service required

    Budget range (optional)

    Project details

    Email, phone, or WhatsApp contact options

Use a clear CTA such as Let's Discuss Your Project.
